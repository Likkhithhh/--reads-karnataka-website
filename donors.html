Our Donors

Our Donors

None of READS' work would be possible without the trust and generosity of our donors — from international partners funding multi-year programmes, to local well-wishers who give their time, resources and support closer to home.

International partner

AEIN — Aide à l'Enfance de l'Inde et du Népal

AEIN, based in Luxembourg, has been a steadfast partner since 2016, supporting READS Ballari's Campaign and Advocacy against Child Marriage and Gender Disparity — an ongoing programme reaching children and young girls across Ballari and Koppal districts.

Their support has helped fund awareness rallies, district-level conventions on child protection, rural entrepreneurship training for women, school outreach with learning materials, and community plantation drives — work you can see more of on our Our Work page.

Local supporters

Well-wishers closer to home

Alongside our international partners, READS Ballari's work is sustained by the generosity of individual well-wishers, local businesses, and community members in Ballari and the surrounding districts. Their contributions — whether financial, in kind, or through volunteered time — help us reach children and families who need it most.

We are deeply grateful to every local supporter who has stood with us. If you would like to join them and support our work, we'd love to hear from you.
