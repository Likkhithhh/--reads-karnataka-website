Our Board & Leadership

Our Board

The leadership of READS is strengthened by a dedicated and experienced Board comprising professionals from diverse fields — social development, education, rural development, women's empowerment, human rights, environmental issues, and community welfare.

With decades of collective experience, our Board provides strategic guidance, promotes transparency and accountability, and supports READS in advancing its mission to empower marginalised and vulnerable communities.

Collective leadership

A shared commitment to lasting change

The READS Board brings together diverse professional experience and a shared commitment to social justice, gender equality, child protection, community empowerment, and sustainable development. Through responsible leadership and collective decision-making, the Board provides strategic direction to READS and supports the organisation in creating meaningful and lasting change in the lives of marginalised communities.

READS
